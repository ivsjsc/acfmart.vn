# ACFMart Seller Verification Policy

## Overview

The ACFMart Seller Verification Policy establishes a multi-layer verification system that ensures trust and authenticity in the marketplace. Unlike other platforms with soft verification, ACFMart implements strong verification requirements to protect buyers from counterfeit products and unreliable sellers.

## Verification Levels

ACFMart implements a 5-layer verification system:

### Level 1: Identity Verification
- **Requirement**: Valid Vietnamese ID card (CCCD) or VNeID
- **Purpose**: Confirm the seller's identity
- **Process**:
  - Upload clear photos of both sides of CCCD/VNeID
  - Facial recognition verification
  - Phone number verification
  - Email verification
- **Timeline**: 1-2 business days
- **Display Badge**: "Identity Verified"

### Level 2: Business Registration
- **Requirement**: Valid business license or equivalent registration
- **Purpose**: Verify the seller as a legitimate business entity
- **Process**:
  - Submit business registration certificate
  - Verification against government databases
  - Confirmation of business address
  - Tax identification number validation
- **Timeline**: 3-5 business days
- **Display Badge**: "Business Verified"

### Level 3: Financial Verification
- **Requirement**: Bank account verification
- **Purpose**: Confirm financial backing and enable secure transactions
- **Process**:
  - Link business bank account
  - Small deposit verification (small amount deposited and verified)
  - Confirmation of business ownership of account
  - Financial capacity assessment
- **Timeline**: 3-5 business days
- **Display Badge**: "Financially Verified"

### Level 4: Operational Audit
- **Requirement**: Random operational audit
- **Purpose**: Ensure ongoing compliance and operational capability
- **Process**:
  - Physical address verification
  - Operational capability assessment
  - Inventory verification
  - Customer service evaluation
- **Timeline**: 5-7 business days
- **Display Badge**: "Operationally Verified"

### Level 5: Brand Authorization (if applicable)
- **Requirement**: Official brand authorization documentation
- **Purpose**: Enable official brand representation
- **Process**:
  - Submit brand authorization letter
  - Direct verification with brand owner
  - Product authenticity documentation
  - Trademark registration verification
- **Timeline**: 7-10 business days
- **Display Badge**: "Brand Authorized"

## Seller Onboarding Process

### Initial Registration
1. Register with email and phone number
2. Complete basic profile information
3. Accept terms and conditions
4. Begin Level 1 verification

### Progressive Verification
1. Complete Level 1 to activate account
2. Access increases with each completed level
3. Receive verification badges and benefits
4. Maintain status through periodic re-verification

### Re-verification Requirements
- Level 1: Every 2 years
- Level 2: Annually
- Level 3: Annually
- Level 4: Every 6 months
- Level 5: Every 6 months

## Compliance Monitoring

### Ongoing Requirements
- Maintain valid contact information

- Comply with product authenticity requirements

### Performance Metrics

## Penalties and Deactivation

### Warning System
1. First offense: Warning and educational resources
2. Second offense: Temporary suspension (7 days)
3. Third offense: Suspension (30 days)
4. Repeated offenses: Permanent deactivation

### Immediate Deactivation Triggers
- Confirmed sale of counterfeit products
- Fraudulent activity
- Misrepresentation in verification process
- Failure to maintain required verification levels
